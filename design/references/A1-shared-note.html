Drivers of north-spit retreat, 1962–2019

Maya Okafor, Daniel Reyes, Priya Natarajan and you · Created Sep 30 · 4 citations

Summary

The north spit at Harlow Inlet has moved landward across the period the catalog covers. This note sets out the drivers the team has found evidence for so far, and where the record is still thin.

1. Channel dredging

The earliest aerial coverage shows a continuous spit with a broad dune line Aerial photograph, 1962. The beach profiles surveyed before any channel work give a usable baseline Profile survey notebook, 1978 · p. 12, and permit filings from the mid-1980s describe deepening the navigation channel along the spit’s inner edge Dredging permit, 1985Priya Natarajan. What is missing is a survey taken after the work, to compare against the 1978 profilesDaniel Reyes
